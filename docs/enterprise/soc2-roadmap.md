# Aegisora SOC 2 Roadmap

Status: Planning.

## Control domains

- access control
- change management
- secure development
- logging/monitoring
- incident response
- backup/recovery
- vendor management
- security awareness
- risk management

## Evidence

Collect:
- CI results
- access reviews
- vulnerability reports
- incident records
- deployment records
- approval records
- vendor assessments

## Audit

Engage an independent auditor/advisor for readiness and the formal examination.

No SOC 2 certification claim should be made before certification is actually achieved.
