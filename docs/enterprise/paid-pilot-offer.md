# Paid design-partner pilot offer

**Status:** commercial working draft; quote and agreement must be signed before work begins. No pilot customer, booked revenue, or performance result is implied.

## Offer

A four-week, paid design-partner engagement to validate one non-production AI-agent workflow at the point immediately before a consequential tool action.

### Included

- One customer-owned non-production workspace.
- One workflow and one framework, only after a technical-fit check confirms the integration is supported.
- Up to three explicit allow/block/escalate policies.
- A controlled test plan using synthetic or customer-approved low-risk data.
- One kickoff, weekly review, and closeout session.
- A closeout packet containing the configured policy set, observed decision outcomes, failure notes, and an evidence export.

### Excluded

- Customer production traffic, sensitive personal/health/payment data, or autonomous execution controlled by Aegisora.
- SLA, uptime, latency, accuracy, ROI, or compliance guarantees.
- SSO/SCIM, dedicated VPC/on-premises delivery, custom integrations, 24/7 incident response, and certification claims.
- Use of customer name, logo, quotations, or metrics in marketing without separate written consent.

The customer retains the final tool-execution decision and must ensure a tool cannot execute unless the customer-side integration receives an `ALLOW` result. `ESCALATE` is not an approval and must pause the action for the customer's human reviewer.

## Commercial terms to put on the signed quote

- Fixed fee and currency agreed for the exact scope before signature.
- VAT/GST/sales tax treatment and invoice issuer verified for the seller's jurisdiction before collection.
- Invoice schedule, due date, payment method, cancellation, and refund terms written in the order form.
- Start date only after both signatures, payment arrangement, technical owner, test environment, data boundaries, and rollback contact are confirmed.

No public list price is set. Competitor positioning is predominantly sales-qualified: Lakera's public site directs enterprise prospects to “Talk to Sales”; this is context, not a price benchmark. [Lakera](https://www.lakera.ai/)

## Qualification threshold

Do not issue a firm quote until the discovery checklist in [pilot-customer-discovery.md](pilot-customer-discovery.md) confirms an isolated workflow, an integration point before tool execution, a technical owner, acceptable data, customer security review, and written success/stop criteria.

Do not begin a product pilot with sensitive data until the hosted membership/audit/MFA migrations, customer data terms, retention/deletion process, production monitoring, and rollback evidence have been completed and reviewed.
